The story of your listening: your eras, the artists you ghosted, the songs you skip but never delete.

How to get your Spotify export (no login needed)
  1. Go to spotify.com → Account → Privacy settings.
  2. Tick Account data (arrives in a few days) and, for skips and your full history, Extended streaming history (can take up to 30 days).
  3. Upload whichever arrives first. When the other one lands, upload it with your profile key and it merges.

Why not "Log in with Spotify"? Spotify caps hobby apps at 5 users. Exports work for everyone.

Your raw files are read in memory and never saved. You can delete everything with one click.
